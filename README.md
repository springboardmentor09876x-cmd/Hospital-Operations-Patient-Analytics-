# 🏥 MedTrack_DV
## Hospital Operations & Patient Analytics Dashboard

MedTrack_DV is an interactive healthcare analytics solution designed to transform hospital operational and patient data into actionable insights.

The project analyzes hospital admissions, discharges, patient flow, department performance, readmissions, bed utilization, staff allocation, equipment usage, and resource capacity using **Microsoft Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor hospital admissions and operational performance
- Track admission and discharge trends
- Analyze average patient stay
- Monitor peak patient load
- Compare department performance
- Analyze department-wise patient volume and readmissions
- Monitor bed utilization
- Track staff allocation and equipment usage
- Analyze resource availability and capacity
- Provide interactive filtering and dashboard navigation

---

## 📊 Dashboards

The project contains four interconnected dashboards.

### 1️⃣ Hospital Overview

Provides a high-level view of hospital performance.

**KPIs**
- Total Admissions
- Average Stay
- Bed Occupancy
- Readmission Rate

**Visualizations**
- Admissions Trend
- Readmission Trend
- Bed Occupancy Trend
- Admissions by Department
- Monthly Admissions vs Discharges

---

### 2️⃣ Patient Flow

Analyzes patient movement through admission, discharge and department transfers.

**KPIs**
- Admissions
- Average Stay
- Discharges
- Peak Patient Load

**Visualizations**
- Admission Trends
- Discharge Tracking
- Average Stay Analysis
- Patient Movement Analysis
- Department Transfer Analysis
- Transfer From/To Department Table

---

### 3️⃣ Department Analytics

Analyzes department-level performance.

**KPIs**
- Department Efficiency
- Total Patients
- Readmission Rate
- Department Capacity

**Visualizations**
- Department Comparison
- Patient Volume by Department
- Readmission Rate by Department
- Treatment Capacity
- Monthly Department Analysis

---

### 4️⃣ Resource Utilization

Analyzes hospital resource usage and availability.

**KPIs**
- Bed Usage
- Total Staff
- Equipment in Use
- Resource Capacity

**Visualizations**
- Bed Utilization by Department
- Equipment Usage Tracking
- Resource Availability
- Staff Allocation Monitoring
- Capacity Planning Insights

---

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboards provide interactive filters for:

- Admission Date
- Department
- Doctor
- Gender
- Hospital Name

These filters allow users to perform focused analysis across different hospital dimensions.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Admissions | Measures hospital patient demand |
| Total Discharges | Measures completed patient episodes |
| Average Stay | Measures average patient stay duration |
| Bed Occupancy | Monitors inpatient capacity utilization |
| Readmission Rate | Identifies repeat patient utilization |
| Department Efficiency | Compares department performance |
| Department Capacity | Supports capacity planning |
| Equipment in Use | Monitors equipment utilization |
| Resource Capacity | Supports resource planning |
